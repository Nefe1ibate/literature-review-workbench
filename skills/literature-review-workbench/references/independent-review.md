# Independent review for consequential syntheses

Use this optional mechanism for central comparisons, contribution wording, delicate methods, or a substantial final manuscript. It borrows selected review discipline from the local Cogentic research-audit workflow. It does not invoke or satisfy that workflow's full theorem/prover/controller protocol.

## Assign actual independent contexts

Give an independent reviewer a fresh agent context when possible, with the review question, frozen manuscript, original sources, domain rules, and relevant source locations. Do not supply another reviewer's verdict, the drafter's defense, or a desired result. Record the actual agent identity and dispatch/context policy when available. Reusing one context under two role names is not independent review. Report missing isolation information rather than inventing it.

Paper reading can be divided between writers. It is not automatically an independent review of the assembled argument. Keep attribution/source support separate from the manuscript's organization and from proving that an external theorem is mathematically correct.

## Freeze the assessed version

Before dispatch, retain immutable copies of the editable manuscript(s), domain rules, instruction resource, and assessed evidence/claim bundle. A receipt records:

- receipt ID, reviewer identity, assessed task and actual reading scope;
- artifact paths and SHA-256 digests of retained assessed bytes;
- source IDs/digests, profile/instruction digests, and assessment date;
- findings tied to manuscript sections or paragraphs and source locations;
- verdict for each scoped finding: accept, repair, or unassessable;
- unresolved original-text, inference, attribution, and coverage questions.

A reviewer must declare output files final/stable before they are registered. Retain that declaration or message and the hashes of delivered files. Hashes identify bytes; they do not establish scientific correctness.

## Repair and recheck

A material edit creates a new assessed version. Preserve the previous version and findings; recheck affected factual claims and prose against current evidence. If a repair changes the research positioning or section objective, check its neighboring argument too. Do not copy a previous acceptance onto a changed manuscript. A scoped recheck may accept a repaired version while explicitly retaining unrelated unknowns.

Before delivery, compare the final editable manuscript, selected profile, and cited source digests with the receipt. PDF export and visual checks are separate: a visually correct PDF does not establish source support, and a source-reviewed Markdown file does not establish that its export was complete.

## Keep the effort proportionate

One fresh final reviewer may suffice for a focused narrative review. Independent source and technical reviewers can improve demanding comparisons when available. Do not impose a universal agent count, proof search, or arbitrary number of rounds. The workspace coordinator may synthesize literature; the Cogentic prohibition on an orchestrator producing mathematical derivations applies only when running its full research-audit protocol.

Retain unresolved findings and assessed denominators. Do not call selective adaptation a complete Cogentic audit, formal proof verification, official DAS evaluation, or demonstrated effectiveness improvement.
